src/machine/apu.rs is a port of Core/apu.c from SameBoy v1.0.5 (commit ea667ad79aa28409f034b1fd6da7304e1844750d,
https://github.com/LIJI32/SameBoy), and the APU circuit in src/apu_circuit/ computes the same rules.
src/machine/fifo.rs, one line's mode 3, ports the same version's pixel fetcher and FIFO (Core/display.c:
GB_display_run from mode_3_start, advance_fetcher_state_machine, render_pixel_if_possible) and its write
timing (Core/sm83_cpu.c, the conflict maps of cycle_write), and the stepper in src/ppu_circuit/stepper.rs
computes the same rules.
